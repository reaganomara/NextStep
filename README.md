# NextStep
NextStep is designed for college students who struggle with health anxiety and become overwhelmed when they notice a physical symptom. The problem is that health anxiety can lead people to repeatedly Google symptoms and seek reassurance because they don't know what they should do next. NextStep allows a user to enter what they are experiencing and answer a small number of safety-focused questions, then provides a clear next step rather than a list of possible diagnoses. AI is used to understand the user's situation and guide them toward an appropriate response, such as seeking professional medical attention when warning signs are present or using anxiety-management strategies when emergency warning signs are not identified. NextStep does not diagnose medical conditions or replace professional medical care.
